What Makes This Market

The Space Coast is the only beach market in the U.S. where rocket launches are a demand driver. With 109 launches in 2025 (record), up to 120 projected for 2026, and SpaceX projecting 44 Starship launches alone — each one draws thousands of launch-watchers who book Cocoa Beach STRs. This creates demand spikes across all seasons, partially offsetting the typical Florida beach seasonality problem. Port Canaveral's 8.4–9M cruise passengers add a second non-correlated demand stream. A 2027 mega-development (5,000 rooms, wave pool, convention center) signals long-term demand infrastructure investment.

What Breaks Deals

Three forces destroy returns in this market: (1) Zoning errors — buying a residential-zoned home west of A1A thinking it's STR-eligible; (2) Condo special assessments — Florida SB 4-D (2022 Surfside law) is forcing massive reserve-funding mandates on older buildings; (3) Insurance sticker shock — beachside combined insurance (HO + flood + wind + STR endorsement) runs $12,000–$18,000/year, 3–4x what pro forma spreadsheets assume. Get actual insurance quotes before any offer. The market is very viable for the buyer who does the homework; it destroys the buyer who doesn't.

Critical Market Alerts

⛔ Zoning Kill Zone — East vs. West of A1A

Cocoa Beach STRs are permitted ONLY in Tourist (T), Commercial (C), or tourist overlay zones — primarily east of SR A1A. Residential zones (R-1 through R-3) in the inland/west sections prohibit STRs entirely. Do NOT assume any property is STR-eligible without written confirmation. Call Cocoa Beach Planning & Development: (321) 868-3238. A single misstep here costs the entire investment thesis.

⛔ Florida Condo Structural Assessment Crisis

Florida SB 4-D (2022, post-Surfside) requires all 3+ story condos to complete structural integrity reserve studies and fund reserves based on findings — by Dec 31, 2024. Many older Cocoa Beach beachfront condo buildings are now facing $30,000–$150,000+ per-unit special assessments. Avoid ANY condo over 3 stories without confirmed Phase I/II structural report, current reserve study, and zero outstanding special assessments. This rule alone eliminated most of the Cocoa Beach condo inventory from this buy box.

⚠ Insurance Sticker Shock — Get Actual Quotes First

Combined coastal insurance (HO Wind/Hurricane + NFIP Flood + STR Endorsement) in beachside Cocoa Beach runs $12,000–$18,000/year for a $600K–$700K SFH. Canal/west-of-A1A properties with lower flood exposure run $7,000–$11,000/year. The standard "35% OpEx" model assumes ~$4K/yr and overstates net CF by $8,000–$14,000/year. Always get actual quotes before submitting any offer. Florida tort reform in 2025 has modestly reduced rates — some improvement expected — but coastal premiums remain elevated.

⚠ Cape Canaveral Supply Explosion — Avoid That Sub-Market

Cape Canaveral STR supply grew 47.2% in the past 12 months — the largest supply increase of any Space Coast sub-market. Average annual revenue in Cape Canaveral is only $24,500 vs. $63K in Cocoa Beach proper, with occupancy of 41.5% vs. 59%. This combination of supply growth + lower revenue makes Cape Canaveral a poor STR investment at current pricing. Stick to Cocoa Beach proper (beachside tourist zone) or established southern zones.

⚡ DSCR Insurance Challenge

Florida's elevated insurance costs can fail DSCR qualification. A $665K property with $12,000–$14,000/yr in insurance + $39,000/yr P&I = $53,000/yr baseline before any other expenses. At only $75,000 gross revenue, your DSCR ratio is 1.41 — which qualifies. But lenders using a "49% expense ratio" will count insurance differently. Confirm with your DSCR lender exactly how they calculate the ratio before locking in a property. Current DSCR rates: 6.12%–6.49% (June 2026) at 20% down.

⚡ Merritt Island 90-Day Minimum — Total STR Prohibition

Brevard County (unincorporated, including most of Merritt Island) requires a 90-day minimum rental period in most residential zones — effectively banning traditional short-term rentals. Only properties in designated "Resort Dwelling" zones are STR-eligible. Do not purchase any Merritt Island residential property for STR purposes without confirmed Resort Dwelling zoning in writing. Merritt Island is off the STR map for this buy box.

How It Works for This Buy Box

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA, 2025) permanently restored 100% bonus depreciation for qualified property placed in service after January 19, 2025. For a $665,000 Cocoa Beach STR:

  • Commission a Cost Segregation Study (~$4,000–$7,000) immediately after closing to identify short-lived personal property (5–7 year items: appliances, flooring, cabinetry, fixtures, landscaping, etc.)
  • Typically 20–25% of a Cocoa Beach SFH purchase price qualifies as short-lived personal property = $133,000–$166,250
  • Apply 100% bonus depreciation: deduct the entire amount in Year 1
  • At 24% federal bracket: $31,920–$39,900 tax savings in Year 1
  • Florida has no state income tax — you keep 100% of the federal savings
  • If you qualify as a Real Estate Professional (750hrs/yr) or the STR qualifies for the short-term rental exception (avg guest stay <7 days), this may offset W-2 or passive income

Consult a CPA with STR/real estate professional experience before purchase. Recommend: confirm professional qualification or passive activity rules with your tax advisor based on your full income picture.
