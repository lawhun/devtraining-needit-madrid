What Makes This Market

Mansfield sits at the intersection of Ohio's motorsport corridor and entertainment tourism. Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course (8 miles from downtown) hosts IMSA, IndyCar, MotoAmerica, and vintage racing events drawing 15K–70K+ attendees per event. The Ohio State Reformatory anchors InkCarceration (90K) and Blood Prison — national-caliber events with no local hotel capacity to absorb demand overflow. AirDNA's top score of 100 confirms the market is statistically exceptional. Entry prices of $145K–$177K mean entire projects can be funded for less than a single down payment in coastal markets, generating outsized CoC returns.

What Breaks Deals

Three landmines: (1) PM dependency — the pro forma requires self-management; 8% PM cuts CoC by 6 points, and 20%+ PM destroys the thesis. (2) Operator quality gap — the top performer earns 2.6x median ($56.6K vs ~$21K), meaning execution quality outweighs market selection. (3) Tax compliance trap — Airbnb does NOT file Richland County lodging tax; self-file monthly or face penalties. Sub-$150K properties carry reno risk that can blow the equity cushion. Zoning must be verified by direct phone call — no codified protection means one council vote could change rules.

Risk Factors & Mitigations
1

4BR Sample Thin (n=4 Meaningfully Booking)

Only 9 total 4BR listings with 4 booking meaningfully. Mean occupancy of 27% across segment masks the performers. Base-case underwriting for 4BR carries statistical uncertainty.

MITIGATION: Anchor Stretch Lane underwriting to Comfort Hub comps (49% occ, $139 ADR) rather than segment mean. Acquire Standard Lane 3BR first; scale to 4BR on second acquisition with live market data.
2

Forward 90d Pacing -9% (Watch Flag)

Forward 90-day bookings pacing 9% below prior-year period. One additional negative data point would trigger a MARKET_PACING_SOFTENING flag. Could indicate demand softening or a seasonal booking-timing shift.

MITIGATION: Monitor AirDNA / AirROI pacing monthly. If two consecutive months show negative pacing, shift pricing to prioritize occupancy over rate to maintain CF floor. Bear scenario ($7,407 CF at 14.7% CoC) is the stress test.
3

Operator Quality Gap — 2.6x Performance Multiplier

Top performer earns $56,604 TTM vs segment median of ~$21,000. A 2.6x gap means execution quality outweighs market selection. Risk of underestimating the operational learning curve, especially during the first event season.

MITIGATION: Invest in listing quality before launch (professional photography, copywriting, amenity differentiation). Pre-load ALL 8 HIGH-tier event calendar rate rules in PMS before first booking. Target 4.8+ stars within first 90 days to secure algorithm ranking.
4

Self-Managed Requirement — No PM Buffer

Pro forma is built on 0% PM. Hiring a PM at 8% compresses CoC from 34.8% to ~28.8% (~$3,100 less per year). At 20%+ PM, the CF thesis becomes marginal relative to risk and capital deployed.

MITIGATION: Structure for remote self-management with a local co-host ($15–20/hr for cleaning oversight and guest issues) rather than full PM. Build PM cost as a known contingency in year-2 planning if lifestyle changes require it.
5

Richland County Lodging Tax — SELF-FILE Required

Airbnb does NOT remit Richland County local excise/lodging tax. This is a non-obvious compliance gap that catches Ohio investors. Failure to file monthly results in penalties and back-tax liability that can exceed $1,000+ annually.

MITIGATION: Register with Richland County Treasurer’s office before first guest check-in. Set up monthly filing via Avalara MyLodgeTax or direct county portal. Calendar recurring due date (20th of following month). Budget 30 min/month for compliance.
6

Reno Surprise Risk on Sub-$150K Properties

Properties below $150K frequently carry deferred maintenance — aging HVAC, roof near end-of-life, electrical panel at 60A. The $12,500 reno budget is calibrated for $160K+ properties; cheaper buys can easily double that exposure.

MITIGATION: Full inspection contingency on every property. Scope mechanical, roof, electrical. Add 20% buffer to any reno estimate for sub-$150K buys. If inspection reveals $20K+ in deferred items, negotiate price reduction or walk. Don’t let a cheap sticker price absorb a $30K reno.
7

STR Insurance Gap — HO Policy is Void During Rentals

Standard homeowner insurance does not cover STR activity. If a claim occurs while Airbnb guests are in-house, the policy will be denied. This leaves the investor exposed to multi-six-figure personal liability without recourse.

MITIGATION: STR-rated insurance required before first guest. Recommended carriers: Proper.insure (STR-native), CBIZ Vacation Rental Insurance. Typical Ohio cost: $1,200–$2,000/year for a $160K property. Build into operating expense pro forma before closing.
8

Zoning — No Codified Regulatory Protection

No STR ordinance as of May 2025 is currently favorable (Ch. 1167 silent = lawful per Ohio case law), but absence of explicit protection means a future council vote could restrict operations. Ohio case law (Russell Twp. 2025) protects established uses, not necessarily new registrations.

MITIGATION: Verify status by phone before every close. Acquire in neighborhoods with multiple active STR listings to establish community tolerance. Begin operating quickly post-close to establish usage precedent for any future grandfather protections.
Regulatory Compliance Checklist
  • ✓
    STR Ordinance Status: No STR ordinance in Mansfield as of May 2025 codification. Zoning Chapter 1167 is silent on STRs. Current status: favorable for new acquisitions.
  • ✓
    Ohio Case Law Protection: Russell Township v. 7722 Fairmount LLC (11th District Court of Appeals, Ohio, 2025) — STRs are lawful in residential districts absent an explicit prohibition in local code. Strong and recent precedent for Mansfield/Richland County operations.
  • !
    Richland County Lodging Tax — SELF-FILE REQUIRED: Airbnb does NOT remit Richland County local excise tax on your behalf. You must independently register with the Richland County Treasurer and file monthly returns (due 20th of following month). Non-compliance triggers penalties and back-tax liability.
  • i
    Ohio State Sales Tax on Lodging: Ohio collects 5.75% state sales tax on short-term lodging; Airbnb handles Ohio state remittance automatically. Richland County local lodging tax is separate and is the operator’s responsibility. Confirm current county rate with the Richland County Treasurer (typically 3–4%).
  • !
    Zoning Verification by Phone (Pre-Close): Before any offer goes non-contingent, call Mansfield Building / Planning Division and confirm: (1) no pending STR ordinance at subject property address, (2) current residential designation, (3) no conditional use permit required. Document the contact name, date, and verbal confirmation received.
  • ✗
    HOA Properties — Hard No: Any property with an HOA must be confirmed STR-friendly in the actual CC&Rs document, not just verbal agent confirmation. HOA enforcement is a top-3 STR failure mode nationally. If HOA exists, obtain written STR permission before removing inspection contingency.
  • ✓
    STR-Rated Insurance (Required Before First Guest): Bind an STR-specific policy effective on closing date. Recommended carriers: Proper.insure (STR-native policy), CBIZ Vacation Rental Insurance. Standard HO policies are void during commercial short-term rental activity. Typical cost: $1,200–$2,000/year.
  • i
    City of Mansfield Business License: Mansfield may require a general business license for rental income activity conducted within city limits. Confirm with City Finance / Income Tax Division. Typically $25–$75/year; often overlooked but simple to obtain.
  • i
    Platform Policies (Airbnb / VRBO): Maintain 4.8+ star rating for search ranking position. Enable Airbnb Host Guarantee. Set cancellation policies to Firm or Strict during HIGH-tier event weekends — non-refundable is optimal for 3-night minimums to prevent last-minute cancellations from event-driven bookings.

30-Day Go-To-Market Roadmap

Sequenced for maximum capital efficiency. Self-managed operator path. Start clock on closing date.

WEEK 1 Days 1–7: Acquisition & Compliance Foundation
1
Confirm zoning by phone before going non-contingent. Call Mansfield Planning Division. Document contact name and verbal confirmation.
Critical path item — non-negotiable regardless of time pressure from seller or agent.
2
Full inspection with STR-specific scope: HVAC age and condition, roof remaining life, electrical panel (60A vs 100A+), plumbing, foundation. Budget $400–$600 for a thorough inspector.
Sub-$150K properties: add mold inspection and sewer scope. Spend $800 on inspection to avoid a $20K reno surprise.
3
Register for Richland County Lodging Tax with County Treasurer. File first monthly return before first guest payout clears. Set calendar recurring reminder for the 20th of every month.
4
Bind STR-rated insurance policy effective on closing date. Proper.insure or CBIZ. Provide policy number to lender for closing package. Do not close without it active.
WEEK 2 Days 8–14: Renovation & Furnishing Sprint
5
Execute fast reno in priority order: fresh neutral paint, deep clean, flooring refresh, kitchen and bath updates. Target 7-day reno window for $160K property with $12.5K budget.
Hot tub installation has a 2–3 week lead time. If hot tub is in the plan, order immediately post-inspection-clear to avoid missing the first compression event window.
6
Furnish for full guest capacity + event groups: Sleeps-8 configuration. Heavy-duty furniture rated for rental use. Extra bedding sets (2x per bed), full kitchen kit, quality towels. Budget $8,000–$14,000 for 3BR/8-sleep SFR.
7
Install smart home stack: Smart lock (August or Schlage keypad — no key handoff), Nest/Ecobee thermostat (remote control), Ring doorbell, Minut noise monitor (Airbnb-compliant, no audio recording).
WEEK 3 Days 15–21: Listing Launch & Rate Strategy
8
Professional photography session when furnishing is 100% complete. 20–30 hero shots. Exterior (day + dusk), every room, outdoor amenities. Photography pays back 3–5x in first month through higher ADR and conversion.
9
Create listings on Airbnb + VRBO simultaneously. Write headline targeting event travelers: "Race-Week Ready — Sleeps 8 · 10 min to Mid-Ohio." Include every HIGH-tier event name (Honda Indy, InkCarceration, AMA Vintage, MotoAmerica) in the description body for search indexing.
10
Pre-load rate rules for all 8 HIGH-tier events in PMS (PriceLabs or Wheelhouse). Set 3-night minimums on Honda Indy, InkCarceration, and AMA Vintage. Rate floor: $172 base; event multiplier: 1.5–2.5x depending on lead time.
Honda Indy 200 (~70K attendance) and InkCarceration (~90K): set rates and minimums now even if the event is 6+ months out. Early rate-locking captures the premium.
WEEK 4 Days 22–30: Review Velocity, Systems & Optimization
11
Generate the first 5 reviews fast. Consider opening at -15% to -20% below target rate for the first 2 weeks to generate booking velocity. Guest review count in first 30 days determines search ranking trajectory on both Airbnb and VRBO.
12
Build local contractor bench: Cleaning team lead (event weekend availability guaranteed in writing), handyman (same-day availability for guest issues), HVAC on-call, plumber on-call. Rate card agreement before first booking.
13
File first Richland County lodging tax return if any guests checked out during Month 1. Verify Ohio state sales tax is being remitted by Airbnb (download monthly statement from platform and confirm line items).
14
30-day post-launch review: Compare actual ADR and occupancy vs pro forma. Adjust base rate if occupancy >70% (room to raise) or <50% (lower to capture bookings). Track forward pacing for next 2 HIGH-tier events.
